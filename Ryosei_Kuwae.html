Ryosei Kuwae - Okinawan Karatedo 1920-1938) OKINAWA

An interesting karate expert, who was also known as the last student of "Bushi" Matsumura Sokon. Kuwae was a very tall individual and was noted for being a kicking expert. The modern-day Kuwae family, descendants of Kuwae Ryosei, still preserve the original scroll given to Ryosei by Bushi Matsumura in the latter's own script. It is believed that Hisataka also studied under Kuwae. Kuwae was also a student of Kyan Chotoku , and accompanied Kyan and Hisataka Kori on their infamous trip to Taiwan. On this trip, Kyan defeated the famous Judo Sensei, Ishida Shinzo, in a challenge match.
