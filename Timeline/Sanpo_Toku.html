	title Timeline of Sanpo Toku
	December 12, 1887 : Born in Kagoshima Prefecture, Japan.
	Elementary school : Started training in Judo, Karate and Kendo
	     : He defeated 165 opponents in a Kohaku shiai
	May 1906 : Moved to Tokyo and joined the Kodokan school
	1912 : Expelled from the Kodokan school after challenging 15 sailors of the Brazilian Navy to a match.
	1917 : Pardoned by Kano and reinstated as a Kodokan teacher
	     : Took Iwazo Hayashi as his pupil.
	1925 : Started training Minoru Mochizuki 
	1930 : Suffers a hard defeat by yoko shiho gatame to Torao Uto.
	Early 1930s : Started training Masayoshi Kori Hisataka
	1932 : Reaches third place in the predecessor league to the modern All-Japan Judo Championships.
	March 10, 1945 : Killed in a Tokyo air raid during World War II.
