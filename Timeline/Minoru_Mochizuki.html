	timeline
	title Timeline of Minoru Mochizuki
	April 7 1907 : Born in Shizuoka, Japan.
	1912 : Began to study Judo and Kendo at his grandfather's dojo in Shizuoka.
	1921 : Moved to Tokyo with his family
	1924 : Joined the Kendokan Dojo of Sambo Toku(Tokusanbo), a noted Judoka and Kendoka of the time.
	     : Started Studying Gyokushin-ryu jujutsu with Sanjuro Oshima
	     : (6 month later) Awarded "Shoden Kirishi Mokuroku" in Gyokushin-ryu which would be roughly equal to Shodan in judo
	1925 : Joined the Kodokan where he became an outstanding competitor.
	June 1926 : Achieved his Shodan in Judo at the Kodokan.<\a>
	1927 : Accepted as Uchi Denshi by Kyūzō Mifune sensei (director of the Kōdōkan, 10th Dan in Jūdō) 
	1930 : Sent by Jigoro Kano to study Aikijujutsu with Morihei Ueshiba.
	1931 : Opened his own dojo in Shizuoka City, which he called the Yoseikan
	     : Masaji  Yamaguchi, a student of Kenwa Mabuni, taught Mabuni’s version of Shito-ryu Karate at Yoseikan
	     : Mokokatsu Inoue taught Ryukyu Kobujutsu at Yoseikan
	1932 : Awarded Menkyo Kaiden and two Daito-Ryu scrolls (“Goshinyo no Te” and “Hiden Ogi no Koto”) by Ueshiba
	1935 : Awarded his 5th Dan in Judo.
	1936 : First son, Hiroo, was born.
	1938 : Received an important government office and moved to Mongolia, became director of the school for Mongols in Paou to.
	1940 : Appointed the Under Prefect of Sei Ga.
	1941 : Shinan Hisataka managing a railway station in a major city in Mongolia where Mochizuki was the district governor. They trained regularly at Butoku-Kai (in Sarachi (Salaqi, Sa-Hsien) or Baotou (Paotou)) and inspired Mochizuki to create "Happo ken no kata".
 	1946 : Returned to Japan
	1950 : Re-organised the Yoseikan Dojo.
	     : Frenchmen Jim Alchiek and Claude Urvois arrived as students at the Yoseikan. 
	1951 : Asked to be part of a Japanese cultural delegation to UNESCO in Geneva.
	     : Travelled to France as a Judo teacher, becoming the first to teach Aikido in the West.
	1956 : Received his 5th Dan in Kendo and Jo Do.
	1977 : Received his 8th Dan in Judo.
	1979 : Received his 10th Dan in Aikido.
	1989 : Began a North American Tour.
	2000 : Received the delegation of the European Yōseikan Budō in Shizuoka
	2001 : Due to his bad health, he moved permanently to Europe, to his son Hiroo in Aix-en-Provence
	Summer 2001 : Passed on the succession as Soke of the Yōseikan to his son Hiroo before the international assembly of the school instructors
	May 30 2003 : Died in Aix-en-Provence, France
