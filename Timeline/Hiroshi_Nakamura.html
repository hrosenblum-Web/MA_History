	timeline
	title Timeline of Hiroshi Nakamura
	Section Early years
	June 22, 1942: Born in Tokyo, Japan
	1954: Began practicing judo, extensively at the Kodokan (Tokyo)
	By late 1960s: Achieved 5th dan and was teaching judo in Japan
	1966: Became Vice-Champion of Japan
	: Dispatched from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Kôdôkan to the Middle East (Egypt, Sudan, Iran) for about a month to spread and develop judo.
	Section Moved to Canada
	1968: Moved to Montreal, Canada, Invited by  Doug Rogers, with the goal of helping develop judo nationally
	: Began offering lessons at Vanier College in Montréal while giving free demonstrations in the cafeteria at lunchtime to spark student interest
	1969: Started coaching Canadian national athletes at World Championships
	1973: Founded the Shidokan Judo Club in Notre-Dame-de-Grâce (NDG), Montreal. It would become the most prestigious and successful judo club in Canada
	1970's and 80s: Coached elite national athletes and developed Quebec’s provincial judo system
	Section Olympics years
	1976: Served as Head Coach for Team Canada at the Montreal Olympics
	1984: Head Coach at the Los Angeles Olympic Games.
	1988: Head coach at the Seoul Olympics
	1992: His student Nicolas Gill wins bronze at Barcelona Olympics
	1996: Head coach at the Atlanta Olympics
	1998: Inducted into the Judo Canada Hall of Fame.
	2000: Head coach at the Sydney Olympics
	: Nicolas Gill wins silver in -100kg
	2004: Head coach at the Athens Olympics, his final Olympic coaching appearance
	Section Advancing Canadian judo
	2005: Continued leading Shidokan, mentoring younger coaches and judoka
	: Served as technical advisor to Judo Canada and international delegations
	: Shidokan became Canada's National Training Centre
	2010: Awarded Foreign Minister's Commendation
	2011: Received Queen Elizabeth II’s Diamond Jubilee Medal
	2013: Received the Geoff Gowan Award Invested as a Member of the Order of Canada
	2014: Shidokan ceases to serve as Canada’s national training center
	October 23, 2019: Inducted into the Canadian Olympic Hall of Fame
	: Awarded the Order of the Rising Sun, Silver Rays for his contribution to the promotion of judo in Canada by the Consulate General of Japan in Montreal
	October 2023: Inducted into Canada’s Sports Hall of Fame, receiving the Order of Sport
	Section Present
	Ranks and Credentials: 9th Dan (Kudan) – Judo Canada, One of only five Canadians to hold the 9th dan rank
	: 8th Dan – Kodokan Judo Institute (Japan)
	: PNCE Level 4, IJF A License (Shiai & Kata)
