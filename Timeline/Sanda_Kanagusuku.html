	title Timeline of Sanda Kanagusuku (Kinjo Sanda)
	1841: Born in Okinawa, Ryukyu Kingdom.
	Unknown date: ?Possibly studied under Ishimine Peichin
	: ?Possibly a student Bushi Matsumura (or according to Choki Motobu just a close friend) and Matsu Higa
	: Mostly trained in Sai but was proficient in bō, eku, tuifa (tonfa), nunchaku, tekko, teko, techu, nuntesu, kama, gusan, sanjakubo, kusarigama, nawa, uchibo, surujin, kyushakubo, nuntesu bo, jingasa, renkuwan, sansetsukun, naginata, tessen, and tanbo
	Late 1800s: Serves as a guard to the last Okinawan king, Sho Tai. During this time, he gains significant experience in weaponry and martial arts.
	: Accompanied Sho Tai to Edo as part of the Ryukyu entourage, serving as the overseer of the royal horses
	: Became a police commissioner (ufuchiku) in Okinawa. which influences the name of his martial arts style, Ufuchiku Kobudo
	Unknown date: Developed and popularized the use of sai among local police, creating techniques based on Te to dodge and hit simultaneously
	: Founded Ufuchiku Kobudo, also known as Ufuchiku Kobujutsu or Ufuchiku-den
	1906: Shosei Kina, one of Kanagusuku's most notable students, begins his training under him
	Around 1920: Masayoshi Kori Hisataka, began his training under Sanda Kanagusuku (after Hisataka's father had passed away)
	1921: Committed voluntary suicide as a tribute to the death of King Sho Tai
	Post-1921: Shosei Kina becomes the second-generation head master of Ufuchiku Kobudo, continuing to teach and spread the art
	: Kina Shosei chose Isa Kaishu(a student of Tokashiki Saburo) to be the Ufuchiku family style head (Soke), making him the third generation inheritor
