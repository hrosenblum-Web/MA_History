	timeline
	title Timeline of Kentsū Yabu
	Section Lived in Okinawa
	September 23, 1866 : Born in Shuri, Okinawa
	As a young man : Learned Shuri-te from Matsumura Sōkon (one of the senseis of Chōtoku Kyan) and Ankō Itosu. 
	March 19, 1886 : Married Takahara Oto (1868-1940).	
	Around 1889 : Together with Motobu Chōyū and Motobu Chōki, learned Tomari-te from Kōsaku Matsumora(another sensei of Chōtoku Kyan). 
	Section Served in the Japanese Army
	December 1890 : Joined the Japanese Army.
	1894 : Served in the First Sino-Japanese War.
	Section Returned to Okinawa
	1895 : Completed his service in the First Sino-Japanese War. Promoted to lieutenant.
	1906 : Became a teacher at Okinawa Prefectural Normal School.
	1908 : Oldest son, Kenden, emigrated to Hawaii.
	1910s : Started training Yabiku Mōtoku (A prominent figure in the development of Okinawan karate)
		: Started training Kanken Toyama (Founder of Shūdōkan karate)
		: Started training Tokuda Anbun (Known for his contributions to the spread of karate)
		: Started training Kyoda Jūhatsu (Founder of the To'on-ryu style of karate.)
		: Started training Gima Shinkin (Played a significant role in promoting karate in mainland Japan.)
		: Started training Makoto Gima (Another influential student who helped spread karate.)
	1912 : Kenden moved to California.
		: Yabu Kenden became known as Kenden Yabe, after a method of transliteration then being used on Japanese passports.
	Section Lived in United States
	1919 : Kenden Yabe married
		: Moved to and demonstrated karate in Hawaii.
		: Visited California to see his son and hoped to see his grandson
	early 1920s : While in Hawaii started training Richard Kim (Founder of Kokusai Butokukai known for his expertise in Okinawan Kobudo and Shōrinji-ryū karate)
	1921 : Kenden's wife became pregnant.
	Section returned to Okinawa
	1927 : Returned to Okinawa via Hawaii after spending nine months in the Territory.
	late 1920s : Started training Kōri Hisataka (Founder of Shorinjiryu Kenkokan Karate)
	1936 : Visited Tokyo and met Shōshin Nagamine, emphasizing the importance of preserving traditional Okinawan kata.
	March 28, 1937 : Attended with Chōtoku Kyan, Maeshiro Choryo, Hanshiro Chomo, Shimpan Shiroma, Choshin Chibana, Mabuni Kenwa and Shinken Taira the meeting to commemorate the establishment of the Kihon-gata in Okinawa (very famous photo)
		: The 12 basic kata were created and acknowledged by the Okinawan Prefecture Promotion Society
	August 27, 1937 : Died at Shuri, Okinawa
