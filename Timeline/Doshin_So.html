	timeline
	title Timeline of Doshin So
	Section Early years
	Feb 10, 1911: Born Michiomi Nakano (中野道臣, Nakano Michiomi) in Mimasaka, Okayama, Japan. Eldest of three children. His father was a customs officer; his mother a seamstress.
	1919: Father died from alcoholism at 30, so he was sent to Manchuria, to live with his paternal grandfather, an employee of a Japanese railroad company and a member of the right-wing Black Dragon Society (黒龍会 Kokuryūkai).  His sisters go to live with their maternal family.
	:  His grandfather was also an expert in kendo, sōjutsu(spear fighting), and most likely Fusen-ryū jujutsu. The grandfather often taught him whenever there was time.
	1926: His mother died so he returned to Japan. His sisters and grandfather would die shortly thereafter. 
	: He is taken under the patronage of his grandfather's friend Mitsuru Tōyama, who was also founder of the Black Ocean Society (玄洋社 Gen'yōsha), the forerunner of the Black Dragon Society.
	Section Military years
	1928: He returned to Manchuria, having enlisted in the army and having joined the Black Dragon Society
	: As a covert reconnaissance activities, he was posted in a Taoist school headed by Chen Lian, a priest who was also the master of Báilián Quán (白蓮門拳 Byakurenmon-ken, "Chinese boxing"). 
	: Chen introduced him to Wen Taizong, grandmaster of Yihe Quán (義和門拳 Giwamon-ken, "Righteous Harmony Fist"). Wen would take him in as his student, training him for many years. 
	Early 1930s: Works as a military intelligence agent, conducting geographic surveys and map-making across China.
	March 1934: Married Takada Toyoko, while he was still training in Henan
	January 10, 1935: Their first daughter, Nakano Tomiko, was born
	1936: Wen formally passed his title of grandmaster of Yihe Quán to Nakano, at the Mount Song Shaolin Monastery, at Henan province. (This has been contested by martial arts historians such as Donn Draeger)
	: Began using the name Sō Dōshin (宗 道臣),
	October 10, 1937: Their second daughter, Nakano Michiko, was born
	January 23, 1941: He divorced Toyoko. 
	August 10, 1943: He married Manabe Michiyo
	1956: He divorced Michiyo by mutual consent
	August 9, 1945: The Soviets invaded Manchuria, and overran the Japanese in 11 days. He was living in Manchuria during the Soviet invasion and witnessed many atrocities during this time.
	1946: He managed to escape to Japan with the help of his friends in Chinese secret societies. Settling in Tadotsu, Kagawa Prefecture, Shikoku.
	Section later years
	October 1947: Established Shōrinji Kempō (少林寺拳法) in Tadotsu, Kagawa Prefecture. This is a combination of the martial techniques he had learned in China with his own innovations and Buddhist philosophy that emphasizes the unity of body and mind ("kenzen ichinyo") and integrates self-defense, mental training, and health practices.
	: He established the doctrine of Kongo Zen, a fusion of Zen philosophy and martial discipline.
	: Changes his name to Doshin So, adopting the on’yomi reading of his given name.
	January 20, 1957: Married Harada Emiko
	November 1, 1957: His third daughter, Nakano Yūki, was born 
	1965: Establishes the Shorinji Kempo Hombu (main headquarters dojo) in Tadotsu, responding to the rapid growth of the movement and increasing number of students.
	1972: The International Shorinji Kempo Federation is created to help organize and manage branches worldwide, setting the foundation for the future World Shorinji Kempo Organization.
	1979: He was invited to China by the Chinese government and asked to reintroduce quan fa to the Shaolin temple, but was not able to complete this task before his death.
	May 12, 1980: Died of heart disease in Tadotsu, Kagawa, Japan.
	: Left his third daughter, Yūki, as his successor.
	2020: Yuuki So (Doshin So II) retires from leadership of the Shorinji Kempo movement
	: Yuuki So’s son, Kouma So, becomes the head of the movement.
